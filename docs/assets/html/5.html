Analysis

Problem Statement

SPCE / MCA / SEM-IV

Many applications are deployed to production without proper security testing. This negligence creates significant vulnerabilities that malicious actors can exploit, leading to severe consequences for organizations and users alike.

The Issue

Common Vulnerabilities:

  • Open ports & weak configurations exposed to public internet
  • Critical flaws like SQL Injection & Cross-Site Scripting (XSS)
  • Higher risk of unauthorized hacking & system compromise

The Impact

Business Consequences:

  • Massive data theft & loss of intellectual property
  • Severe reputational damage & loss of customer trust
  • Legal penalties & fines (GDPR, ISO non-compliance)
